# 0.9.0 delivery plan

Sequencing for the train after 0.8.0. The ROADMAP holds *what* each item is and the evidence behind
it; this holds *when it can be done and what blocks it*, because 0.9.0's defining property is that a
large share of it is not ours to unblock.

Measured on 2026-09-01, against the tree at the 0.8.0 cut.

## The hinge: two upstream cuts

Neither `exeris-kernel` nor `exeris-sdk` has a final `0.12.0` — both exist only as
`0.12.0-SNAPSHOT` in the local repository. The release rule that has bitten this repo twice
(`v0.5.0`, `v0.6.0`) applies in advance here: **no cross-repo dependency may be a SNAPSHOT at a
cut**, so nothing in Track B or Track C can even be pinned, let alone released, until those two
repos cut finals.

That is not a reason to wait. Track A is a full train's worth of work with no external gate, and
running it first means the bump slice arrives at a tree that is otherwise finished.

## Track C — behind an SDK record change

Each of these is a carrier that does not exist, not an extraction somebody forgot. Extracting into
nothing is the failure mode this repo spent 0.8.0 removing.

- **`GraphEdgeMetadata` needs the field and the identity as separate components** — until then, two
  `@GraphEdge` on one field are refused at the declaration (S3). `name` is currently both the edge's
  identity and the source of the entity getter.
- **`@Saga.compensation*`** — six attributes on the annotation. Three (`Strategy`, `Order`,
  `Timeout`) have carriers and no extraction; three (`MaxRetries`, `RetryDelay`, `Dlq`) have no
  carrier. What an emitter would *do* with `BEST_EFFORT` or `PARALLEL` is a separate kernel-contract
  question that has not been measured.
- **`@SagaStep.waitForAll` / `.failFast`** — no carrier, and blocked below as well.

## Blocked on a kernel contract, not on us

- **`@SagaStep.parallel`** — extracted, carried, read by no emitter. The emitted flow is a strict
  linear chain because `FlowDefinition` cannot express concurrent steps, so the chain is the only
  correct compilation available. **Deliberately not in `INERT_ATTRIBUTES`**: an entry would record a
  missing kernel contract as generator neglect, and would be unreachable anyway — `warnInertAttributes`
  has no `Saga`/`SagaStep` call site.

## Adjacent, worth doing whenever it is convenient

- **Two missing `warnInertAttributes` call sites** — `Saga` and `SagaStep`. Independent of whether
  any saga attribute ever earns a registry entry; today strict mode can say nothing about either
  annotation's attributes. Same shape as the standing `T11-strict` marker on `DomainEvent`.
- **A comment naming the processor as the saga-step sorter.** `KernelSagaGenerator`'s Javadoc says
  callers must sort; nothing on the processor side says it is that caller. Two independent readers
  took it the other way in consecutive PRs.

## Design-gated, unchanged by this plan

`EV2` (`@EventSourced` aggregates), the behavioural AST family (`@Derived`, `@Rule`,
`@EventHandler`, `@Projection`), and the full `@View` emitter (G1/G2/G3/G6). All three are waiting on
a decision or a corpus, not on an extraction.

## Suggested order

1. **A2 → A3** — two small defect closures, each with its own evidence, while the upstream cuts are
   out of our hands.
2. **A4** — decide store-vs-service, then emit.
3. **A5**, then **A6** after its design call.
4. **A7's RFC** — the largest item here and the least dependent on anything else, so it can fill any
   wait; write it early rather than last.
5. **A1** once that RFC fixes T51's direction, since it is the direction that decides whether D10 is
   a deletion or a wiring.
6. **B0** the moment kernel 0.12 is final, then the gated cluster in the order above.

A1 was first in the version of this plan that went up for review, which contradicted a standing
ROADMAP instruction one file away. Caught in review; the ordering above is the correction, not a
clause papering over it.

Track C stays open until the SDK moves; nothing in it should be started here first.
