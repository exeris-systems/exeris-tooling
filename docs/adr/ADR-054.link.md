# ADR-054 — Field/Validation constraint-scoping cut (link stub)

**Authoritative copy:** [`exeris-sdk/docs/adr/ADR-054-field-validation-min-max-pattern-cut.md`](https://github.com/exeris-systems/exeris-sdk/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-054-field-validation-min-max-pattern-cut.md)

ADR-054 is a **cross-repo** decision owned by `exeris-sdk`, shipped in SDK **0.9.0**. It closes the overlap deferred since 0.2.0: `@Validation` is the **sole declaration site** of `min` / `max` / `minLength` / `maxLength` / `pattern`, `FieldMetadata` is the **sole AST carrier** of those values, and `ValidationMetadata` — a record no processor ever populated and no generator ever consumed — was removed outright rather than deprecated, since there was no published artifact for anyone to depend on.

> Cross-repo link-stub: the authoritative copy lives in the repo named above; the URLs resolve to their canonical GitHub locations. Convention catalogued in `exeris-docs/adr-index.md` → *Cross-repo stubs*.

## Why this stub exists — the lockstep was tooling-side

The decision is an SDK-surface ruling, but it could not land alone. Three of its consequences were this repo's to absorb:

- **The SDK pin.** `ValidationMetadata` disappearing is a compile-level break for anything importing it; the pin bump to `0.9.0` is what carries the new AST shape in.
- **The dead TS mirror.** `exeris-codegen-ts` carried a `ValidationMetadataSchema` zod mirror of a record nothing produced — the TS side of the same orphan, removed with it.
- **The schema skew.** ADR-054 moved `SchemaVersion.CURRENT` to `"0.9.0"` for the per-component `NON_NULL` boxed-zero fix on the `FieldMetadata` bounds. Since the processor stamps `schemaVersion` into `exeris-metadata/<entity>.json` (ADR-042), every pre-existing baseline read as skew until a one-time codegen re-run re-stamped it.

The boxed-zero fix is the part worth remembering here: under class-level `@JsonInclude(NON_DEFAULT)`, Jackson 3 treats a boxed `0` as empty and drops it, so a legitimately-zero bound (`minLength = 0`) vanished on the wire and read back as absent. The four bounds carry per-component `@JsonInclude(NON_NULL)` for exactly that reason — a generator reading them is reading values that now survive a round trip.

## When to consult ADR-054 from tooling

- Reading constraint values in the processor — they come from `@Validation` only. `@Field` declares shape and lifecycle (`required`, `inCreate`, `inUpdate`) and **no** constraint attributes; do not add a `@Field`-side read, and do not reintroduce a parallel AST validation carrier.
- Deriving DB `NOT NULL` / not-blank semantics — those are **derived** from `FieldMetadata.required` at generator level, not separately declared anywhere.
- Extending the zod field schema in `exeris-codegen-ts/src/models/domain-model.ts` — it mirrors `FieldMetadata`; the constraint fields live there and nowhere else.
- The `@Validation.required` / `@Validation.validateOn` fallback path (`applyDeprecatedValidationFallbacks`) — both attributes are `@Deprecated(forRemoval = true)` and the fallback-with-warning window runs until SDK 1.0.0.

## Engineering status

ADR-054 is **ACCEPTED** (2026-07-21); the SDK side merged 2026-07-22 (`exeris-sdk` **#82**). The tooling lockstep is **complete**: `exeris-tooling-bom/pom.xml` pins `exeris.sdk.version` at `0.9.0` (`43d7d84`, 0.7.0 G0), the `ValidationMetadataSchema` mirror is gone (no `ValidationMetadata` reference remains in this repo), and `ExerisDomainProcessor` populates the `FieldMetadata` bounds from `@Validation` with the deprecated-fallback path kept separate and warned. This stub is recorded retrospectively — the registry row was `cross-repo` from the start, but the stub was never filed.
